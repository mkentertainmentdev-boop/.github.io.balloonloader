Privacy Policy - Balloon Loader

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Developer: Mohammad Kasim

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Balloon Loader ("the app", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. You do not need an account to play, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts.

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins and settings such as music and sound) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Your device's Advertising ID
  • IP address (which can indicate your approximate location)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size)
  • Information about ad views and interactions
  • Diagnostic and crash information related to ads

This data is collected and used by Google according to its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and user data

3. Internet connection check

Before showing a rewarded ad, the app may contact a Google address (clients3.google.com/generate_204) only to check whether an internet connection is available. No personal information is sent with this check. The game itself works offline.

5. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID / opt out of personalised ads: on Android go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Turn off music and sounds any time from the pause menu.
  • Delete all game data: uninstall the app, or clear its data in Settings → Apps → Balloon Loader → Storage. Since we store nothing on our side, there is nothing else to delete.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security

Because the app does not collect personal information on our side, we do not store or transfer any of your personal data. Data processed by Google AdMob is protected according to Google's security practices.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
